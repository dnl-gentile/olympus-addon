# Olympus 1.6.3

Public addon release authorized by Daniel on 23 November 2026. Real signing-key
setup and private service activation remain separate. Existing Verify approvals,
the seven-day guild-master grace and the paused warning rollout are preserved.

## Changes

- Personal Discord verification is the first action in The Realm. One owned popup
  gives the steps and Submit code; no copy/refresh detour, implicit submission or
  claim that a local delivery result grants Discord access. Native GUIDs require a
  current complete own-guild roster. Link opens only when installed and explicitly ready.
- Shared-tool participation now covers the additional group, Board, position,
  layer/hop, war, weekly signup and multiplayer paths. Cleanup, local practice,
  census, approvals and authority recovery remain available.
- Scoped public signing certificates support native staff removal observations,
  Council pardons, harboring suspension/clearance and Treasurer milestone edits.
  Private keys stay local; the public package contains no real certificate or seed.
  Decisions, revocation, replay handling and private recovery are independently checked.
- Charity receipt review includes settled native mail and retained native history,
  with explicit Treasurer attestation for existing ledger lines. Balance, deduplication,
  backup protection, anonymous defaults and opt-in public names remain separate.
- Treasurer can publish or withdraw up to four informational milestones with an
  authorized signing key. An expired delegated revision cannot restore the older text.

The candidate also includes the preceding reviewed local AU realm-identity fix,
PR119 repeated realm-link guard and the exact Akafoxslayer portrait/chat artwork.
The portrait conveys no rank or capability. Those changes were independently
checked at commit ba89abd0 before the Fern completion work.

## Authority limits and real-client acceptance

The harboring grace is 24 hours with two fresh observations and a visible
daily notice. Council decisions last at most seven days; short delegated operator
observations cannot outlive their 30-minute grant. Long-lived sanctions require
Council renewal or a reviewed complete FUG1 list. These are explicit safety choices
for deployment, not claims of permanent bans or instant delivery to offline clients.

See [the authority design and setup](FEDERATION-AUTHORITY.md),
[participation and contributions](FEDERATION-PROGRAM.md) and
[legacy complete lists](FEDERATION-FUGITIVES.md).

The offline harness exercises real addon functions with simulated native APIs.
Current installed-client verification remains required for native removal identity,
cross-guild transport, Discord submission, roster/role changes and owned-dialog
focus across gamepad transitions. Native guild removal and gold transfer remain
attended game actions. No live or in-game acceptance is implied by an offline pass.

Private Link/backend work is reviewed separately, outside this public repository.
Automatic website feed and attended Army QR activation have their own quorum,
privacy, key/configuration and acceptance gates; a public addon release does not
activate them. No private Link source is included in the public candidate.
